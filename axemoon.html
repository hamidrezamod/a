For the Axemoon project, a family pet photography studio, the design needed to reflect a more playful and imaginative visual language that matched the studio's identity.
Instead of relying on promotional headlines, a storytelling approach was used to introduce the business. This helped create an emotional connection, build trust, and naturally communicate the value of the studio's services.
